## Baseline and measurement rules

- Search baseline is July 20–October 19: 33 clicks, 1,253 impressions. It is not a current 30-day forecast. Recent equal 28-day periods were 8/289 and 17/618; CTR was approximately flat. Establish a fresh matched period after any actual release.
- Keep Search Console country/query/page visibility separate from GA4 engagement, Calendly records and paid-client records. Attribution is incomplete and consent-dependent. Use a voluntary “How did you hear about us?” conversation in normal intake, not extra tracking or personal information in URL parameters.
- GA4 account review should verify that `service_click`, `booking_click`, `diagnostic_click` and `generate_lead` each mean what the owner expects. Do not promote all of them to equivalent business outcomes or claim cross-host attribution is working.
- Russian paths remain untracked. Use GSC Russian-page visibility and owner-reported source information; do not silently enable Russian measurement. The diagnostic has a separate origin and consent review requirement.
- No numerical client/revenue uplift is promised. At this volume, one client can materially change percentages. Prefer counts, relevant qualitative feedback and capacity fit; reassess over longer comparable periods.
- All publication, outreach, purchases, account changes, future tracking or site implementation require the appropriate specific approval. This calendar itself sends nothing and schedules no background work.

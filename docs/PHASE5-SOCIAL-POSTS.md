## LinkedIn 1 — International experience without starting over

POST TYPE: Practical guidance for internationally trained professionals.

COPY-PASTE LINKEDIN POST

When you move to Canada, your previous work still belongs on your resume.

What may need changing is the explanation around it.

A Canadian employer might not know your former company, what your department did, or how much responsibility you had. Give the reader enough context to understand the work.

Start with three questions:

• What did the organization do?
• What were you personally responsible for?
• Which examples matter for the role you want now?

“Responsible for documentation” leaves a lot to guess. Naming the documents, what you checked and who used the information makes the experience easier to assess.

You do not need a percentage in every bullet. Use a result when you can support it. Otherwise, explain your contribution clearly.

I put together a practical guide to adapting international experience for a Canadian resume:
https://hiremenowresumes.ca/international-work-experience-canadian-resume.html

Try it with one experience entry and one target posting. Keep the employer, dates and facts accurate, then make the relevant work easier to understand.

## LinkedIn 2 — Foreign job titles and accuracy

POST TYPE: Practical guidance about job-title clarity.

COPY-PASTE LINKEDIN POST

Changing a job title to sound more familiar in Canada can make a resume less accurate.

For example, training colleagues does not necessarily mean you held a management role. Coordinating office work does not automatically make “Operations Director” a suitable replacement for your actual title.

Start with the title you held, or a faithful English translation. If the function is unclear, add a short explanation.

Fictional example:
Senior Administrator (office coordination)

Then explain the work: scheduling appointments, maintaining records, training colleagues on a process—only if those were your responsibilities.

If you did manage a department, keep that responsibility visible too. There is no need to downgrade an overseas role just because the title is unfamiliar.

The wording should stay consistent with your employment history, LinkedIn profile and interview explanation.

My guide includes examples of clarifying foreign titles without overstating authority:
https://hiremenowresumes.ca/explain-foreign-job-titles-canadian-resume.html

Take a look at one title on your resume. Does it accurately describe both the role and the level of responsibility you held?

## Facebook — A clearer experience bullet

POST TYPE: Educational before-and-after example.

COPY-PASTE FACEBOOK POST

“Responsible for documentation.”

That line tells me very little about the work. What documents? What did you do with them? Who relied on the information?

Here is a fictional editing example—not a client story:

Before:
Responsible for documentation.

After:
Checked customer order forms for missing information and followed up with the sales team before orders were passed to dispatch.

The second version gives the reader something concrete to understand. It names the documents, the action and the process.

It does not add a bigger job title or an invented achievement.

Only use wording like this if it describes work you actually did. You do not have to add a number to make every bullet useful.

I explain this approach in my guide to presenting international work experience on a Canadian resume:
https://hiremenowresumes.ca/international-work-experience-canadian-resume.html

Choose one vague bullet from your resume and ask those three questions. You can work through the guide yourself. If you would like help editing a targeted resume, the article also links to the scope of the Resume Strategy Package.

## Russian Facebook adaptation — international experience

POST TYPE: Практический разбор для русскоязычных специалистов, ищущих работу в Канаде.

COPY-PASTE FACEBOOK POST

«Отвечала за документацию» — такая строка в резюме почти ничего не объясняет человеку, который не знаком с вашей прежней работой.

Какие документы? Что именно вы проверяли? Кому передавали информацию?

Разберём вымышленный пример. Это не история клиента и не результат работы с реальным резюме.

Было:
«Отвечала за документацию».

Можно описать точнее:
«Проверяла полноту данных в заказах клиентов и уточняла недостающую информацию у отдела продаж перед передачей заказов в службу отгрузки».

Теперь понятны действие, документы и место этой работы в общем процессе. При этом мы не добавили человеку руководящую должность или достижение, которого не было.

Такую формулировку можно использовать, только если она соответствует вашему опыту. Не нужно придумывать проценты, чтобы каждая строка выглядела убедительнее.

Опыт, полученный за пределами Канады, стоит сохранять в резюме, если он помогает показать соответствие выбранной вакансии. Иногда достаточно объяснить, чем занималась компания и за что отвечали лично вы.

В статье я разбираю этот подход подробнее. Статья на английском:
https://hiremenowresumes.ca/international-work-experience-canadian-resume.html

Попробуйте начать с одной общей фразы в своём резюме. Добавьте конкретику, которую сможете спокойно объяснить на собеседовании.

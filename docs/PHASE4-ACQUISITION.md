## Free/public employment support is a different category

[JVS Toronto](https://www.jvstoronto.org/home/) explicitly offers free employment support, including newcomer services. [Toronto newcomer kiosks](https://www.toronto.ca/community-people/moving-to-toronto/after-you-arrive-checklist/newcomer-services-kiosks/) provide free/confidential settlement support. These can be responsible alternatives for people who need free assistance. They are **not verified channels for private paid-service referrals**. TRIEC mentoring is also not a private-service sales funnel.

Do not ask publicly funded organizations to steer clients away from free entitlements or imply that a paid resume package is required for employment support. Any educational collaboration must respect their mandate, procurement/referral rules and participant choice. Treat complementary private support as optional and separately agreed. No referral fee or reciprocal-link arrangement is proposed.

Common questions

What to know before you start

What is the difference between writing and reviewing a resume?

The $149 session offers feedback and an action plan but does not include document writing. The $349 and $549 packages include written resumes and cover letters.

Can I get resumes for two different roles?

Yes. The $549 Multi-Path Career Strategy includes two targeted resumes and two tailored cover letters for two role directions or a career pivot.

Do you work with clients outside Toronto?

Yes. Services are provided remotely across Canada.

Can you help with international work experience?

Yes. We help internationally trained professionals present their experience clearly for Canadian employers while keeping the facts accurate.

Does an ATS-friendly resume guarantee interviews?

No. Employers and screening systems differ. Our focus is on readable formatting, relevance and accurate content, not guaranteed outcomes.

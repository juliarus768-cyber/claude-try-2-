- Career Clarity Session, $149 CAD: resume and LinkedIn review, job search pattern review, a clear 2–4 week action plan, target-role and positioning feedback. This is review and strategy guidance, not resume or cover-letter writing. The action-plan period is not a document delivery timeline.
- Resume Strategy Package, $349 CAD: strategy consultation, one targeted resume, one tailored cover letter, LinkedIn positioning guidance, application direction, interview positioning notes and practical job search resources.
- Multi-Path Career Strategy, $549 CAD: strategy consultation, two targeted resumes, resume rewrite or refinement based on target direction, two tailored cover letters, positioning for two role directions, transferable skills messaging, LinkedIn guidance, multi-target application strategy, interview preparation connected to the selected career path, ongoing feedback during the strategy process, and offer evaluation and negotiation guidance.

Do not equate LinkedIn guidance with a full profile rewrite or interview positioning notes with the separate Interview Preparation service. Preserve both credentials: Certified Career Practitioner and Certified Resume Strategist (CRS).

## Appropriate FAQs

- What is the difference between resume review and resume writing? Explain the $149 review and the $349/$549 writing options.
- Can I target two career directions? Explain the verified two-resume/two-cover-letter package.
- Can clients outside Toronto use the service? Explain remote availability across Canada.
- Can you help present international experience? Explain accurate Canadian-market positioning without altering facts.
- Does an ATS-friendly resume guarantee interviews? No; explain readable formatting and role relevance without outcome guarantees.

Do not add unsupported testimonials, employer endorsements, success rates, refund terms, revision limits or delivery promises.

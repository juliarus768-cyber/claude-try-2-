What is Hire Me Now? Hire Me Now is a Canadian career coaching service led by Julia Cher, a Certified Resume Strategist with 15 or more years of experience. The service helps newcomers to Canada, international students, career changers, and professionals who are not getting interviews. Services include career diagnostics, job search strategy packages, resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, and interview preparation. Hire Me Now offers multilingual support in Russian, Spanish, Hebrew, Cantonese, Bengali, Hindi, and Urdu. How does career coaching work in Canada? Career coaching at Hire Me Now involves a diagnostic review of your resume and job search strategy, followed by targeted positioning and execution support tailored to the Canadian labour market. What does a Certified Resume Strategist do? A Certified Resume Strategist helps job seekers create targeted, employer-ready resumes, clarify their career positioning, and build a focused job search strategy aligned with current hiring expectations. Is Hire Me Now based in Toronto? Yes. Hire Me Now is based in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, and serves clients remotely across Canada and North America. Who is Julia Cher? Why job seekers get stuck

The problem is not always your experience

Many qualified job seekers apply again and again without understanding how employers are reading their resume, target roles, and overall positioning.

Strong background, low response

You may have the right skills, but your resume does not clearly connect your experience to the roles you are applying for.

Unclear positioning

When your target role is too broad, employers struggle to see where you fit. You need a clear direction and a stronger value message.

Too many applications, too little strategy

Applying to dozens of roles without a focused plan often leads to frustration. A targeted approach helps you apply with more purpose.

Interview message is not clear

Even strong candidates lose momentum when they cannot explain their value, career story, or fit for the role in a clear way.

Our approach

The Hire Me Now Method

A practical four-step process that helps you move from confusion to clarity, stronger positioning, and a more focused job search.

1

Diagnose

Review your resume, LinkedIn, goals, target roles, and current job search patterns to find what is blocking results.

2

Position

Clarify where you fit, what roles make sense, and how to communicate your value in a way employers understand.

3

Build

Create stronger resume, cover letter, LinkedIn, and application messaging aligned with your target direction.

4

Execute

Apply with a clearer plan, prepare for interviews, and adjust your strategy based on real market feedback.

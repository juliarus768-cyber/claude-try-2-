What is Hire Me Now? Hire Me Now is a Canadian career coaching service led by Julia Cher, a Certified Resume Strategist with 15 or more years of experience. The service helps newcomers to Canada, international students, career changers, and professionals who are not getting interviews. Services include career diagnostics, job search strategy packages, resume writing, LinkedIn optimization, and interview preparation. Hire Me Now offers multilingual support in Russian, Spanish, Hebrew, Cantonese, Bengali, Hindi, and Urdu. How does career coaching work in Canada? Career coaching at Hire Me Now involves a diagnostic review of your resume and job search strategy, followed by targeted positioning and execution support tailored to the Canadian labour market.

Why job seekers get stuck

The gap between
experience and opportunity

Most candidates aren't lacking credentials. They're missing the right strategy for how hiring actually works in Canada.

Strong background, low response

Experience alone isn't enough if your resume and positioning don't match local employer expectations. Formatting, keywords, and tone all matter in Canada.

Unclear positioning

When your target role is too broad, employers struggle to see immediate fit. You need a sharp, specific value proposition tailored to your target sector.

Application overload

Sending dozens of applications without a clear strategy reduces both quality and confidence. A focused, targeted approach consistently outperforms volume.

Interview preparation gaps

Even highly qualified candidates can underperform without clear, role-relevant communication practice. Canadian interview style has its own expectations.

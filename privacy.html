Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2026

Hire Me Now collects personal information such as your name, email address, and payment information when needed to deliver career coaching and resume strategy services. We use this information to communicate with you, manage bookings, and deliver purchased services.

Hire Me Now may share limited information with trusted third-party service providers such as Calendly and payment processors to operate booking and payment workflows. We do not sell your personal information.

Under PIPEDA, Canadian users can request access to personal information, ask for corrections, and inquire about how their information is used. To make a privacy request, contact info@hiremenowresumes.ca.

If you have any privacy questions, please contact info@hiremenowresumes.ca.

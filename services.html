The ideal starting point if you are applying but not getting the response you expected. We review your resume, LinkedIn presence, target roles, and current job search strategy, then identify what needs to change.

Language, delivery & policies

Language support

Julia personally provides consultations in English and Russian. Resumes and cover letters are primarily prepared in English for the Canadian job market. Other advertised languages—Spanish, Hebrew, Cantonese, Bengali, Hindi, Urdu, French and Ukrainian—may be supported by established certified employment practitioners, subject to availability. These practitioners consult and work directly with assigned clients and prepare documents; Julia reviews and finalizes the documents. Standard package prices are the same regardless of consultation language.

With Julia directly, a first writing draft is usually ready within two business days after the paid consultation. Work involving external consultants typically takes 5–10 business days; a two-day first draft is not promised for that work.

Revisions and files

Resume-writing packages include revisions for six weeks from delivery of the first draft, with no fixed number of rounds for the agreed job title or career directions. Requests made within that period remain covered even if completion takes longer. Later updates are available for a smaller, separately quoted fee; a new job target requiring new strategy or documents is a separate paid service. Provider errors and outstanding agreed deliverables are corrected without extra charge. Final resumes and cover letters are editable Microsoft Word (.docx) files.

Booking, payment and cancellation

Start with a complimentary 15-minute fit call. The $149, $349 and $549 standard packages require full payment before the paid consultation and work begins. We accept Interac e-Transfer and PayPal. Advertised prices include HST.

Refunds for standard packages

Before the paid consultation, a full refund is available. For the $349 and $549 writing packages, if you cancel after the paid consultation but before the first resume draft is delivered, $149 is retained for the completed consultation and the balance is refunded. After the first draft, no refund is offered for voluntary cancellation or change of mind, subject to applicable consumer protection rights. For the $149 Career Clarity Session, the fee is non-refundable for a change of mind after the full 45-minute consultation; the promised written summary and action plan remain included.

Rescheduling and missed appointments

Please give at least 24 hours' notice to reschedule. Emergencies may be considered individually. Missed appointments without notice are assessed individually; any rebooking fee is communicated before a new appointment is confirmed.

These business policies do not override statutory cancellation or refund rights.

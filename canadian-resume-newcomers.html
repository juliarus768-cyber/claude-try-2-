Make your experience easier to understand

An unfamiliar employer name, a job title used differently in Canada, or a long list of duties can make relevant experience harder to assess. Start with a target role, then choose evidence that helps the reader evaluate your fit. You do not need to hide where you worked or replace your professional history with entry-level language.

Canadian professionals and career changers can also use our professional resume writing services. This page focuses on adapting international experience.

Adapt your resume to the role

CV conventions vary between countries, industries and employers. For many Canadian non-academic roles, a concise resume focuses on relevant experience, skills and achievements. Academic CVs and some specialized applications need more detail; follow the posting rather than treating one format as universal.

Usually, leave out a photograph, date of birth, marital status and unrelated personal details. Use clear headings and a straightforward layout. Check the requested file type and application instructions. Read our guides to resume photos and design in Canada and ATS-friendly resumes.

Explain employers, titles and credentials accurately

  • Employers: add a short factual description if the organization may be unfamiliar, such as “regional food distributor.” Use a scale or market description only when you can verify it.
  • Job titles: keep the actual title and add a plain-English explanation in parentheses when needed. Describe the function without upgrading seniority.
  • Responsibilities: explain the work in terms the target employer uses: customer inquiries, purchase orders, scheduling or reporting, for example.
  • Education: list the credential, institution and country clearly. Describe an assessment only if completed and accurately reflected in its report. Do not claim Canadian equivalency or professional licensing without evidence.

For regulated occupations, resume wording cannot replace the registration or licensing required for a role.

Show transferable skills with evidence

Compare a posting with your own experience. If it asks for coordinating schedules, resolving customer issues and maintaining records, identify where you actually did those things. Use the employer’s terminology when it accurately describes your work. Keywords cannot substitute for a qualification or experience you do not have.

Write about your contribution and its context. Use numbers only when you can support them; a clear description of a process improved or a problem resolved can be useful without a percentage.

Illustrative wording example

This fictional example shows an editing approach, not a client result. Use it only if the details match your experience.

Before: Responsible for work with clients and documents.

After: Coordinated customer order inquiries, checked supporting documents and followed up with warehouse colleagues to resolve delivery questions.

The revision names the work and the people involved. It does not add an invented achievement, Canadian employer or performance figure.

Frequently asked questions

Can I include work experience from outside Canada?

Yes. Relevant international work belongs on your resume. Explain the employer, your responsibilities and evidence of your contribution so readers can assess its relevance to the target role.

Should I translate my job title?

Use an accurate English rendering and, if needed, a short explanation of the function. Keep the level and scope truthful; do not substitute a more senior title just because it appears in a posting.

Can I use the same resume for every posting?

Keep a master record of your experience, then tailor the emphasis to each suitable role. A package covers its agreed job title or directions; a new target requiring new documents is separately quoted.

Will a new resume guarantee interviews?

No. Hiring decisions depend on many factors, including role fit, qualifications and employer needs. Professional support helps you present your experience clearly; it cannot guarantee interviews or employment.

Julia Cher

Certified Resume Strategist · 15+ Years Experience

Julia Cher leads Hire Me Now with a practical, human approach to career strategy. Her work is grounded in one core belief: that most job seekers aren't lacking talent — they're missing the right system for the market they're in.

Having worked with hundreds of clients across Canada, Julia has developed a deep understanding of how hiring works at every level — from entry-level positions to senior leadership roles — and across a wide range of industries and backgrounds.

She is known for helping clients make clear decisions under uncertainty, communicate their value with precision, and build a job search process that is sustainable, not exhausting.

What guides us

Our approach

Clarity

We start by helping you see your situation clearly — what's working, what isn't, and exactly what needs to change. Vague advice doesn't move careers forward. Clarity does.

Strategy

Every recommendation we make is grounded in how the Canadian job market actually functions — not how people assume it works. We build strategies that hold up under real conditions.

Results

We measure success by one thing: whether our clients get interviews and ultimately accept offers they're proud of. Everything we do is oriented toward that outcome.
